# Testing

Automated checks and physical acceptance answer different questions. The 0.4.10 R3 offline suite exercised a simulated browser and native transport with the actual HA pool/coordinator, including two recovery incidents within one HA call, phone soft mute, stale or delayed capture, playback epochs, owner/auth/DND/clock changes, busy state, truncated frames, late acknowledgements and bounded diagnostics. The review also covered capture fencing and privacy boundaries. These fixtures do not establish the cause of the original physical failure or prove real TLS reconnection, an audible chime, LED behavior or acoustic quality.

One earlier physical speaker test produced human-reported clear two-way audio. Rapid physical mute/unmute later failed on an earlier build. The 0.4.10 rapid physical test and real native recovery remain unproved. Do not treat source publication, a green simulation, an online room or a software progress counter as physical acceptance.

Before an authorized physical test, establish a fresh authenticated HA session, exact target, correct reviewed build and version, device/room availability, no active call or pending cleanup, and an inspectable matching backup and recovery path. Use a trusted HTTPS caller and explicit room permission. Record the environment, timestamps and expected versus observed results without recording conversation or secrets.

For a bounded owner-supervised test, start Talk and Listen, confirm speech in both directions, then use the physical speaker mute button and confirm capture stops and the mute indicator changes. Unmute during the same call: observe the fresh chime and blue indication before capture returns, and confirm two-way speech. After stable audio, repeat the physical cycle; test hangup and confirm idle and microphone/indicator cleanup. Record any interrupted call, duration and displayed error. Do not assume a later pass explains an earlier failure. Further tests for overlap/AEC, interruption, rollback, WAN-independent local access and longer soak require their own arrangements and evidence.

An HA administrator may inspect **Intercom Manager → Diagnostics**. The bounded call records separate server observations from client-reported metadata; reports and writes are best-effort, and last events can be lost. No audio is intended to be stored. Redact household details and never upload credentials, keys, speech or private configuration.

Test file names and commands must be checked against the actual published source tree and installed dependencies. The earlier internal fixtures used a pinned Python/test environment and Node, including a documented test-only polling adjustment; no portable all-platform command or device acceptance is asserted here. Run unmodified release-appropriate checks where their dependencies and test inputs are documented, and report any unavailable fixture or failed check rather than replacing it with a passing claim.
